## 🛠 Features

This collection includes three core modules built for research workflows.

### Research Proposal Builder
This tool helps you structure your ideas. Fill in your project goals, methodology, and expected results. The software organizes your text into standard academic formats. Export your work to PDF for your supervisor or grant board.

### Medical Imaging Review
View and annotate complex imaging files without technical training. The viewer supports common high-resolution formats. Use built-in tools to mark regions of interest. Save your annotations directly to the image file for your patient records or study notes.

### Paper Slide Deck Creator
Convert your written research into visual presentations. Import your existing text or data files. The software applies templates designed for academic conferences. Adjust your layout, add charts, and prepare your talk in minutes.

## ⚙️ How to Use the Modules

Each module works with your existing files. Choose the tool from the main dashboard when you open the program.

### Working with Proposals
Open the proposal module. Create a new project folder. Save your drafts often. The app keeps a history of your changes if you need to revert to a previous version. Use the export button to save your final document.

### Viewing Medical Images
Load your imaging files via the file menu. Use your mouse to zoom and pan across images. Click the annotation button to highlight specific areas. These tools help you track changes over time if you compare images from different clinical sessions.

### Creating Presentations
Select the slide creator icon. Choose a layout that fits your project. Drag your research data into the slide area. Save your progress as a project file so you can return to it later. Export your files as PowerPoint or PDF documents.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about this software package.

**Where do my files save?**
The software saves files in a subfolder within your Documents library. You can change this path in the settings menu.

**Can I use this tool offline?**
Yes. You do not need a live connection to work on your proposals, images, or slides. You only need the connection to fetch software updates.

**Does this software track my personal data?**
No. All your data stays on your local machine. No external servers receive your research notes or clinical images.

**What happens if the program closes unexpectedly?**
The software includes an auto-save function. It stores a temp file every five minutes. Upon restart, look for a prompt to restore your unsaved work.
